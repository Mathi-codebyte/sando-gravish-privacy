Privacy Policy for Sando: Gravish

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Sando: Gravish (“the game”) is a physics puzzle game for Android, published by Alfa Osmosian (“we”, “us”).

The short version: we do not run any servers and we do not collect personal information ourselves. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device data to deliver and measure those ads.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no chat. We do not operate any servers.

Information stored on your device

WhatWhy
Game progress: cleared levels, stars, coins, lives, medals, daily streaksSo you can continue where you left off
Settings: music, sound, vibration and notification preferencesTo remember your choices

This data stays in the game’s private storage on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → Sando: Gravish → Storage, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for hints or extra help. To serve, personalise (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about your device and the app, such as model, OS version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads SDK

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required, for example in the EEA and UK, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

All data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Permissions the app requests

PermissionPurpose
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATELoad ads. The game itself is fully playable offline.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)Optional local reminders, for example when your lives are refilled. These are created on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time.
VIBRATEHaptic feedback during play. It can be switched off in the game’s settings.

Children’s privacy

Sando: Gravish is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share any personal information. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google AdMob, as described above.

Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove everything the game has stored, uninstall it or clear its storage. To manage data held by Google for advertising, visit My Ad Center.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new “Last updated” date.
